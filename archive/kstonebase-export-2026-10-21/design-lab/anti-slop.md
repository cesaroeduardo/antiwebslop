# Anti-slop and design dials

Local skill: `.cursor/skills/web-design-taste`.

## Design Read (mandatory)

Before code: *"Reading this as: <page> for <audience>, with a <language> language, leaning toward <stack>."*

## Dials

Default marketing: `VARIANCE 8 / MOTION 6 / DENSITY 4`.

| Context | V | M | D |
|---------|---|---|---|
| Minimal / Linear | 5–6 | 3–4 | 2–3 |
| Premium consumer | 7–8 | 5–7 | 3–4 |
| Experimental | 9–10 | 8–10 | 3–4 |
| Analytics dashboard | 3–4 | 2–3 | 6–8 |

## Bans

- Purple→indigo gradients / AI glow
- Cream `#F4F1EA` + terracotta + generic editorial serif
- Centered hero + three equal feature cards
- Cards inside cards; stickers on the hero
- `transition: all`; loops without `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Inter/Roboto/Arial as the only display choice for new marketing

## Composition (marketing)

One job in the first viewport; brand-first; full-bleed hero; `min-h-[100dvh]`.

## Product shells

Authenticated apps: keep the project design system. Apply anti-slop to new surfaces / explicit redesigns only.
